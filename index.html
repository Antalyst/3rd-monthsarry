♥ Dear Lalove,

Happy 4th monthsary, Love. ❤️

Four months with you, and somehow, it already feels like we've been through so much together. These past months haven't always been easy. We've had our fights, misunderstandings, struggles, and moments when things felt heavy. We've both made mistakes, and there were times when loving each other wasn't as easy as we wanted it to be.

But despite all of that, you're still here. And I want you to know how much that means to me.

Thank you for staying by my side. Thank you for choosing me, even on the days when I wasn't the easiest person to love. Thank you for your patience, your understanding, and for giving us chances when things weren't perfect. I know I still have a lot to learn and a lot to improve, but I promise I'm trying not just for myself, but for us.

I don't want a relationship where we never fight or never struggle. I want a relationship where, no matter how difficult things get, we continue to choose each other, communicate, grow, and find our way back to one another.

Every day, I thank God for bringing you into my life. My prayer for us is still the same that He continues to guide us, helps us understand each other better, and gives us the strength to overcome whatever challenges come our way.

I also pray that we both reach our dreams, build the careers we want, and grow together without losing ourselves along the way.

And Love, I want you to know this from my heart: I don't see you as someone temporary in my life. I want you to be my last. I want to keep choosing you, keep growing with you, and someday, when the time is right, I want you to be the person I marry and spend the rest of my life with.

I can't promise that we'll never fight or that everything will always be perfect. But I can promise that I'll keep choosing you, keep working on myself, and keep fighting for us.

Thank you for these four months, Love. Thank you for staying. Thank you for choosing me.

Here's to us, to everything we've overcome, and to all the years I hope we still have ahead of us.

Always yours, Andrew ♥
